The Problem This Solves

Most homeowners make six-figure decisions
with almost no objective information.

Home inspections tell you what's broken.

They don't tell you what's possible, what it will cost to fix it, in what order to address things, or who you should trust to do the work. A home inspection is a compliance checklist. It's not a plan.

Contractor estimates are sales documents.

The person advising you on your kitchen is the same person who profits from building it. That's not guidance — that's a pitch. The recommendation you receive is shaped by what the contractor wants to sell, not necessarily what's in your best interest.

Design consultations start at $5,000.

And the plans they produce exceed the average client's budget by 40%. You spend money to learn you can't afford what you thought you wanted. The pricing reality comes after the design commitment.

Lead gen sites sell your information.

HomeAdvisor and Angi sell your contact information to five contractors who then compete on price and volume — not quality. You become the lead, not the client.

The Home Clarity Report is none of these things. It's a 27-year Summit County remodeling expert who walks your home with no financial stake in what you choose to do next. His only incentive is giving you the right answer.

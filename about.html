The Story

Why I Built This

After 27 years in the trades, I kept seeing the same pattern: homeowners making five- and six-figure renovation decisions without the information they needed.

I started AK Renovations in 1999 — just me, a truck, and a commitment to doing honest work in Summit County. Over the years, I built kitchens in Hudson, first floors in Fairlawn, bathrooms in Merriman Valley. Hundreds of projects. Thousands of conversations with homeowners.

And I kept hearing the same things: "We got four contractor bids and they were all different — how do we know who to trust?" Or: "We started the kitchen but now we realize we should have done the bathroom first." Or: "We spent $80,000 and we're not sure we made the right choices."

The problem wasn't the contractors — it was that nobody was helping these homeowners make sense of their home before the work began. They had no map, no roadmap, no advisor.

The Home Clarity Report is my answer to that problem. It's a 65-page assessment, roadmap, and planning package — everything I'd want if it were my home. Architect-grade floor plans. A complete 3D model. A prioritized project schedule with realistic cost ranges. A lifetime advisory relationship.

One visit. Five business days. One point of contact — me — for life.

This is the service I wish every homeowner had access to from the day they bought their home.

"Your home should work for your life. My job is to help you see it clearly — before you spend a dollar."

— Adam Kilgore

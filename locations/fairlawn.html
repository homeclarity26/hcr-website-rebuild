Common Questions

Fairlawn homeowners ask us:

I just want to do a kitchen renovation. Is the full Report necessary?

In our experience, "just the kitchen" rarely stays just the kitchen once you're in the middle of it. Opening walls reveals electrical work, plumbing that needs updating, and structural surprises. The Report identifies what's there before a contractor does — so you're not making expensive decisions mid-project. Yes — and you'll often find it's most clarifying at this stage. The Report gives you an independent assessment of what the scope should include and what it should cost.

What You Get

Eight deliverables.
One visit.

The Home Clarity Report is the most comprehensive home assessment available to homeowners in Northeast Ohio. Here's everything included in your $4,500 investment.

01
30–50 Page Written Report

A prioritized project roadmap with realistic cost ranges and detailed recommendations for every room and system in your home. Not a template — a strategic plan built from your specific home and goals.

02
Architect-Grade Floor Plans

Full interior measurements accurate to ⅛ inch across every level of your home. The same precision architects and designers require, included in your report.

03
Full Exterior 3D Model

Professional-grade measurements and a complete 3D model of your home's exterior, captured via Hover technology. Invaluable for roofing, siding, and exterior renovation planning.

04
Immersive 360° Photo Tour

A virtual walkthrough of every room, captured with Matterport and Insta360 technology. Revisit your home's current state from anywhere, anytime — perfect for contractor consultations and remote planning.

05
Proactive Replacement Pricing

Current, local pricing for every major system — roof, windows, HVAC, water heater, and more. Know the real costs before you get a single sales pitch.

06
Complete Owner's Manuals

Every appliance and mechanical system documented — model numbers, serial numbers, manuals, warranty dates, and recommended maintenance schedules. Your home's permanent operating record.

07
Detailed Scopes of Work

For every remodeling project discussed during your visit, a written scope of work with specifications that you can hand directly to contractors. No ambiguity, no miscommunication.

08
Interactive Home OS Dashboard

Your private client portal with lifetime access. Track projects, reference your report, store documents, and manage your home's entire lifecycle from one place.

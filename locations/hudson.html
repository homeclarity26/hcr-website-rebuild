Common Questions

Hudson homeowners ask us:

I already have contractor bids. Is it too late for the Report?

Not at all — and you'll often find it's more valuable at this stage. The Report gives you written scopes to compare against what contractors have proposed. In our experience, Hudson homeowners who use the Report to validate bids discover meaningful discrepancies more often than not.

My home is historic. Can Adam actually assess it accurately?

Yes — and that's exactly where the Report's value is highest in Hudson. Adam has direct personal experience with historic properties, including his own 1834 farmhouse restoration. He understands what conditions are typical for older construction vs. what requires intervention — and he won't produce a standard code-checklist that misrepresents the character of the home.

Will the Report add more value if we plan to stay long-term vs. sell within a few years?

Both scenarios benefit significantly, just differently. Long-term owners get a prioritized 10-year investment roadmap — what to do, when, and in what order — so every dollar goes into the property strategically. Sellers get documentation that supports list price, reduces buyer negotiation leverage, and eliminates surprises at inspection. Hudson homes command strong prices, and documented condition is worth real money in a transaction.

Common Questions

Bath Township homeowners ask us:

We're planning a major renovation. Can the Report help us sequence it correctly?

Yes — and this is one of the highest-value uses of the Report. Adam identifies mechanical systems approaching end-of-life, structural considerations that affect finish work sequencing, and the order that minimizes cost and rework. Renovating the primary suite before you know the HVAC needs replacement, for example, is an expensive mistake the Report prevents.

We already have a contractor we trust. Why would we need this?

Even trusted contractors benefit from a written scope. It eliminates scope creep, change order disputes, and pricing ambiguity. Bath Township clients who use the Report alongside their contractors report smoother projects and fewer surprises — because everyone is working from the same documented baseline.

Do you work with homeowners who want to phase projects over several years?

Yes — and this is one of the most common planning approaches in Bath Township. Adam builds a multi-year project roadmap that sequences major investments across 3, 5, and 10-year horizons, timed to system replacement schedules and the homeowner's renovation priorities. The Report is designed for exactly this kind of long-horizon planning.

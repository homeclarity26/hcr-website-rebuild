FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your visit.

What exactly is the Home Clarity Report?

The Home Clarity Report is a comprehensive home assessment and planning package created by Adam Kilgore — a licensed general contractor with 27+ years of experience in Northeast Ohio.

Adam visits your home for several hours, assesses every room, system, and appliance, then delivers 8 deliverables within 5 business days: a 65-page written report, architect-grade floor plans, a full 3D model, a 360° photo tour, scopes of work for priority projects, realistic cost ranges, a vetted vendor database, and lifetime access to a private digital portal with all your documents.

It's everything you need to make confident decisions about your home — before you spend a dollar on renovation.

How much does it cost?

The Home Clarity Report is $4,500, one-time. This includes all 8 deliverables and lifetime advisory access to Adam. There are no ongoing fees or hidden costs.

Most homeowners who invest in the report recoup the cost in savings on their first project — by understanding exactly what needs to be done, in what order, and at what realistic cost.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Northeast Ohio — primarily Summit County and surrounding areas, including Hudson, Bath Township, Cuyahoga Falls, Fairlawn, Richfield, Silver Lake, Stow, Copley, Merriman Valley, Peninsula, and Akron.

If you're outside this area and interested in the service, call us at (330) 203-1331 to discuss options.

How long does the home visit take?

The home visit typically takes 3–5 hours, depending on the size and complexity of your home. Adam works methodically through every room, measuring, photographing, and documenting everything.

You're welcome to follow along and ask questions — many homeowners find the visit itself to be educational. Or you can go about your day and let Adam work — whichever you prefer.

When do I receive my deliverables?

All 8 deliverables are delivered within 5 business days of your home visit, typically sooner for smaller homes. You'll receive access to your private portal the same day delivery is complete.

What's the difference between this and a home inspection?

A home inspection (for real estate transactions) focuses on identifying defects and safety issues for a buyer. It's typically limited in scope and written for a different audience (the buyer's agent and lender).

The Home Clarity Report is a planning and advisory document written for the homeowner who intends to stay and invest in their home. It covers not just what's wrong, but what the priorities should be, what order to tackle them in, what it will realistically cost, and who to call. It's a roadmap, not just a checklist.

Do I need to do anything to prepare for the visit?

Not much. Just make sure all areas of the home are accessible — including mechanical spaces, attic access (if safe), and crawl spaces. If you have existing renovation plans, old inspection reports, or any permits on file, it's helpful to have those available, but not required.

Can I use this report to get contractor bids?

Yes — that's one of the primary uses. The scopes of work included in your report are written in contractor-ready language. You can hand them directly to any contractor for a bid and get true apples-to-apples comparisons instead of deciphering wildly different bid formats.

Is Adam available after the report is delivered?

Yes. One of the core features of the Home Clarity Report is the lifetime advisory relationship. Adam is available by phone or email for questions related to your home — whether that's vetting a contractor's bid, thinking through a renovation sequence, or deciding whether a repair is urgent.

This isn't a call center. It's a direct relationship with Adam, ongoing, for as long as you own the home.

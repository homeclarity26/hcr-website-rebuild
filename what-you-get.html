01

Written Report (65 Pages)

The strategic foundation.

Your home, on paper — fully documented. The written report is a 65-page assessment covering every room, system, and appliance in your home. It includes a prioritized renovation roadmap with realistic NE Ohio cost ranges, a project sequence recommendation, and actionable next steps. Every word is specific to your home — not a template.

02

Architect-Grade Floor Plans

Accurate to the inch.

Measured and drawn to professional architectural standards. These aren't hand-sketched room layouts — they're the kind of drawings contractors use for bidding and permitting. Know your exact square footage, ceiling heights, and structural elements before anyone picks up a hammer.

03

Full 3D Model

See every angle of your home.

A complete 3D digital model of your home's exterior. Explore different renovation scenarios before you commit to anything. See how a second-story addition would look, or how a new roofline might change your curb appeal. This becomes your reference tool for every project.

04

360° Photo Tour

Your home, documented forever.

A professional 360° photography walkthrough of your entire home — every room, every corner, every detail. This creates an immutable record of your home's condition at the time of assessment. Invaluable for insurance claims, renovation planning, and future resale.

05

Scopes of Work

Ready-to-bid project specs.

For every priority project identified in the report, you get a detailed scope of work document — the same specs a general contractor would use to bid a job. Take these directly to contractors for apples-to-apples bids. Stop guessing why bids are different.

06

Realistic Cost Ranges

Real numbers, not guesses.

Cost estimates for every identified project, based on actual current contractor pricing in Northeast Ohio. Updated quarterly. These aren't national averages — they're the numbers Adam's network of vetted trade partners would charge for your specific home.

07

Vetted Vendor Database

Access to Adam's network.

Access to Adam's curated directory of trusted Northeast Ohio contractors — electricians, plumbers, HVAC specialists, cabinet makers, tile installers, painters. Every entry is personally vetted. Know who to call before you need to call someone.

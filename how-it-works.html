Step 01
30 Minutes · Free · No Obligation

The discovery call — a real conversation about your home.

Before anything else, we talk.

Adam asks about your home: when it was built, what you love about it, what you're worried about, and what projects have been on your list. He asks about your timeline and your goals — not just for specific projects, but for the home overall.

This is not a sales call. Adam is not trying to close you on the Report. He's trying to understand whether the Report is genuinely the right fit for where you are right now.

If it is, you schedule the site visit. If it's not — if you have a single specific project and just need a contractor referral, or if your situation calls for something different — Adam will tell you that and point you toward what actually makes sense. Free advice is free. The 30 minutes is valuable either way.

What to have ready:
  • A list of your home's current concerns and project ideas
  • Any past inspection reports or contractor estimates you've received
  • A sense of your timeline and your available budget range
  • The other person in the household who makes home decisions (bring them, or loop them in)

Step 02
2 to 3 Hours · At Your Home · Once

The on-site visit — where the real work happens.

This is the only part that happens in person, and it only needs to happen once.

Adam arrives at your home with professional equipment and a systematic approach to documenting every inch of your property. He's not filling out a form — he's building a complete picture of your home from scratch.

Exterior documentation

Adam scans the exterior of your home using Hover technology, capturing precise measurements of every surface — roof facets, siding panels, windows, doors, soffit, and fascia. You'll have a full 3D exterior model within 5 business days.

Interior walkthrough

Every room, every level, every closet, every storage space. Adam measures each room and draws the floor plan as he goes — accurate to ⅛ inch. He photographs everything: finishes, fixtures, conditions, and anything noteworthy.

360° photography

Throughout the visit, Adam captures immersive 360° photos with Matterport and Insta360, creating a virtual tour of your home that you can access from any device, forever.

Systems inspection

Every mechanical system is inspected and documented. HVAC: age, model, condition, filter status. Water heater: age, type, anode rod status. Electrical panel: amperage, breaker condition, any safety concerns. Plumbing: supply pressure, visible drain condition, any signs of past leaks. Roof: visible condition from any accessible vantage points. Insulation: visible coverage in attic and basement if accessible.

Project discussion

Adam walks through every project on your list — the ones you're planning, the ones you're considering, and the ones you're worried about but haven't addressed. He asks specific questions, shares what he sees, and begins forming the recommendations that will appear in the Report.

What to have ready for the visit:
  • Any past inspection reports or project estimates
  • Access to all major mechanical systems (HVAC unit, water heater, electrical panel, attic and crawl space if accessible)
  • Your questions — write them down in advance if it helps
  • The person who makes home decisions with you (this is worth having them present)

The visit takes 2 to 3 hours. Some homes take closer to 2. Larger homes or homes with more complex project lists may take the full 3 or occasionally a bit longer.

Where We Work

Summit County — primary service area.

Hudson

Summit County's premier community. Homes range from historic downtown properties to newer construction. We've assessed dozens of Hudson homes across a wide range of styles. The Report's pricing guidance is particularly valuable in Hudson because home values are high and project costs follow accordingly. Every dollar spent in a Hudson home should be placed with a clear plan.

Bath Township

Large lots, premium construction, high project values. Bath Township homeowners often use the Report when planning significant work — additions, full first-floor transformations, major exterior projects. Getting the sequence and scope right before committing $150,000 is well worth $4,500.

Fairlawn

One of Summit County's most active renovation markets. Kitchen and bathroom work dominates here. Fairlawn homeowners tend to be ready to spend — they just want confidence they're spending correctly. The Report gives them exactly that before they sign anything.

Richfield

Quiet, strategic, long-term oriented. Richfield clients often use the Report for 10-year planning rather than immediate projects. The sequencing and replacement pricing sections resonate strongly here. Know what's coming before it surprises you.

Cuyahoga Falls

Our home base. 27 years in this community. We know Cuyahoga Falls homes at every price point and every age — from starter homes to established estates. If you're here, you're getting advice from someone who has seen the inside of more Cuyahoga Falls homes than anyone.

Stow

Growing market with a wide range of home ages and styles. Stow homeowners face the same challenges as anyone in Summit County — aging systems, contractor uncertainty, project sequencing. The Report provides the clarity that expensive decisions require.

Copley

Established residential neighborhoods with active renovation programs. Copley homeowners typically have specific project lists and want to know exactly what's realistic before committing to contractors. The scopes of work and pricing guidance pay immediate dividends here.

Silver Lake

Small, high-value community. Silver Lake homeowners are thoughtful investors in their properties. Precision planning and vetted contractor introductions are especially valued here — where the homes are worth the extra care.

Montrose-Ghent & Peninsula

Unique character homes that benefit from thorough documentation and planning. Historic structures, large lots, and distinctive architecture require the kind of careful assessment Adam provides. We've worked throughout the Cuyahoga Valley corridor.
